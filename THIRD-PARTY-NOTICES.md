## Google Fonts online fallback

When an Arial Narrow or Calibri PDF subset is missing replacement glyphs, the
editor can download Roboto Condensed or the metric-compatible Carlito family
from the official Google Fonts repository and embed it in the edited PDF.
Roboto Condensed and Carlito are licensed under the SIL Open Font License.
The downloaded font retains its own copyright and license metadata.
